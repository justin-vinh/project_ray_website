Submit Your Design

You imagined it, you built it — now let's turn it into a real toy! Upload a few photos of your model below and we'll take it from there.

How it works

  • 1 Make your toy model using clay, dough, or drawing.
  • 2 Take 3–5 photos of your model from different angles.
  • 3 Fill out the quick form below and upload your photos.
  • 4 Our volunteer makers turn it into a real 3D printed toy!
  • 5 Pick it up at your clinic in about 3 weeks.
Note: designs must fit within a 200 × 200 × 200 mm cube (about 8 × 8 × 8 inches) to be 3D printed.

    Terms of Participation: Toy Collection

    The Toy will be completed within 3 weeks of submission. The Toy must be collected within 4 weeks of completion, or it will be donated.

    Terms of Participation: Contact Info

    No information about you will be collected by default. However, sharing contact info can help coordinate Toy pickup and communications. By contacting Project Ray, you elect to connect personal information with your submission (e.g. email).

    Optional Contact Info

    Completely optional — but sharing your name and email lets us notify you when the toy is complete!

    Toy Safety Disclaimer: While Project Ray will do our best to ensure proper toy design and safety, by submitting this form you acknowledge that the ultimate decision regarding safety and suitability rests with the guardians of the toy designer.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When will I get my toy?

    About 3 weeks after you submit your design.

    Where do I pick it up?

    Usually at the same hospital or clinic where you got your design kit.

    Is my privacy protected?

    Yes — submissions are anonymous, so share as little or as much as you'd like.

    How much does it cost?

    Nothing at all. Project Ray is donation-supported.
