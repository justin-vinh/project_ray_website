Submit Your Design

You imagined it, you built it — now let's turn it into a real toy! Upload a few photos of your model below and we'll take it from there.

How it works

  • 1 Make your toy model using clay, dough, or drawing.
  • 2 Take 3–5 photos of your model from different angles.
  • 3 Fill out the quick form below and upload your photos.
  • 4 Our volunteer makers turn it into a real 3D printed toy!
  • 5 Pick it up at your clinic in about 3 weeks.
Note: designs must fit within a 200 × 200 × 200 mm cube (about 8 × 8 × 8 inches) to be 3D printed.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will I get my toy?

About 3 weeks after you submit your design.

Where do I pick it up?

Usually at the same hospital or clinic where you got your design kit.

Is my privacy protected?

Yes — submissions are anonymous, so share as little or as much as you'd like.

How much does it cost?

Nothing at all. Project Ray is donation-supported.
